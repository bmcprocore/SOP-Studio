Section 01 · Overview

Purpose, scope and roles

This SOP defines how Wardle turns a signed proposal into a live Head Contract in Procore. The Budget is cost; the Head Contract is revenue (marked-up). The two link through cost codes but represent different things — keep them separate, every project, no exceptions.

Applies to All Wardle projects, Concrete & Formwork (primary work category). Australian SoPA 1999 (NSW) jurisdiction.

Purpose & outcomes

Every won job must reach Head Contract status Approved before progress claims start. Margin lives inside the Schedule of Values rates, never as a visible margin line. At end-of-project: HC To Claim = $0 and Progress Claims ≥ Contract Value. Approved claims push Procore → Intify → Xero for invoicing.

Scope

In scope: create the Head Contract, attach signed copy, populate SOV via CSV import (marked-up), move status Draft → Approved, configure Billing View columns, billing periods and the customised progress claim cover sheet.
Out of scope: building the estimate, building the Budget at cost, issuing individual progress claims, post-Approval HC Variations, and subcontract creation — each covered by its own SOP.

Section 02 · Procedure (1 of 2)

Send to Budget → Approved Head Contract

Steps 1–4 cover the handoff from won proposal into a live, Approved Head Contract. Run within 24 hours of signed acceptance — Adam's standard: "that's basically a live project from there."

Reference tool Procore — Head Contract, Budget, Schedule of Values, CSV Import
  1. 01

    Send estimate to Budget (at cost)

    Estimator (Urban) Procore · Estimating → Budget ~10 min
    • Open the won estimate. Click Send to Budget to push every line into the Budget tool.
    • Delete estimating adjustment rows (tax / overheads placeholders) — Brendan: "that's our tax, that's our overheads. They don't exist."
    • Confirm every cost line is present at cost. Budget = cost only. Margin is never baked into the Budget.
    Hard rule "Your budget should be your cost. You don't want to hide it [margin] into those figures." — Margin lives only in the Head Contract SOV. Loading margin into the Budget breaks the Billing View and corrupts every variation quote downstream.
  2. 02

    Create the Head Contract record

    Estimator → PM Procore · Head Contract ~15 min
    • Project Tools → Head Contract → Create Head Contract. Status starts at Draft.
    • Header: Number auto (HC-001); Title = project street address; Owner/Developer from Directory; Contractor = Wardle Construction Pty Ltd.
    • Description of Works — plain-English scope. Contract Start/End per program. GST 10%. Retention 0% default; set per-project where withheld.
    • Attach files: signed proposal PDF, Wardle T&Cs PDF, and (where applicable) client's builder contract.
    • Inclusions / exclusions field: "As per attached contract." Detail lives in the PDF.
  3. 03

    Populate Schedule of Values via CSV import

    Estimator Procore · Head Contract → SOV ~25 min
    • In the Budget Import Template, apply the markup: Budget Amount × 1.30 (or the per-project margin agreed by the Director).
    • Head Contract → Schedule of Values → Import to SOV → Download CSV import template.
    • Populate columns: A Sub Job · B Cost Code · C Cost Type · D Description (blank) · E Tax Code = GST · F Marked-Up Value.
    • File → Save As → CSV (Comma delimited). Confirm .csv extension — not .xlsx. Upload via Import to SOV.
    • Verify in the Billing View: Head Contract column = marked-up total; HC Progress Claims = $0; HC To Claim = HC total.
    Paste values When pasting marked-up values into columns A, B, C and F, use Paste → Values Only. Per the Budget Upload & SOV Import SOP: "formulas may not survive the CSV conversion and will result in blank or incorrect values."
  4. 04

    Approve & transition status to Approved

    PM → Director Procore · Head Contract → Status ~20 min

    PM runs the pre-approval checklist: SOV total reconciles to the signed proposal value; sub jobs match the proposal sections; signed proposal + T&Cs attached; inclusions read "as per attached contract"; GST 10%; retention correct; billing period set. Director (Adam) reviews margin reconciliation and the attached signed copy, then moves status to Approved.

    Why status matters The Billing View only pulls Head Contract values where status is Approved or Complete. Draft and Void are excluded. A Head Contract left in Draft is invisible to reporting and blocks progress claims, Intify push and Xero invoicing — cash flow stops.

Section 03 · Go live & controls

Billing periods, cover sheet, workflow & sign-off

Target state End of project: HC To Claim = $0 · Progress Claims ≥ Contract Value.
  1. 05

    Set billing periods

    PM (Stephen) Procore · Head Contract → Billing Periods ~5 min

    Default: monthly, 1st → end of month. Project-by-project — some clients claim fortnightly, some pour-by-pour. Even when Wardle claims on completion of work, a billing period must be set so costs bucket correctly.

  2. 06

    Customised progress claim cover sheet

    Office Admin (Marsha) → Procore Custom Solutions One-time configuration

    Include: Wardle logo, ABN & contact; client, project address & number; contract sum; previous claims; this claim (ex-GST, GST, total); retention if applicable; Wardle bank details; Building & Construction Industry Security of Payment Act 1999 (NSW) reference; authorised signatory.

    Exclude: "Certified by" fields, Procore default branding, labour/material breakdowns visible to client, internal budget codes, retention columns when retention is zero.

  3. 07

    Configure progress claim approval workflow

    PM → (Director) → Client → Intify → Xero Procore · Workflows 5 business days

    PM creates claim (Draft) → Under Review → optional Director review for high-value → Client approves in Procore or by email. On Approved, Intify pushes to Xero and the invoice is created. Client window 5 business days; day 5 reminder, day 7 escalation, day 10 Deemed Approved under SoPA 1999. Client options are Approve or Send back for adjustment — no Reject by design.

Common issues & resolution

Ref Issue Resolution
ERR-01 CSV upload rejected by Procore. File saved as .xlsx instead of .csv. Re-save as CSV (Comma delimited) and re-upload.
ERR-02 SOV lines blank after import. Formulas survived the CSV conversion. Re-paste columns A, B, C, F as Values Only, save and re-upload.
ERR-03 Billing View shows $0 against an active project. Head Contract left in Draft. Billing View only pulls Approved or Complete — run pre-approval checklist; Director moves status to Approved.
